Settings




Keys: WASD / arrows to drive, Space or Shift for nitro, R to re-centre on track, P or Esc to pause. Player 2 (local): arrows + Right Shift for nitro.

How to play

Drive 3 laps, beat the AI, earn credits, and spend them in the Garage. Draft behind rivals for extra speed, use nitro on straights, and stay on the road — grass, gravel and dirt slow you down and walls damage your handling.

Modes: Quick Race, Career (8 rounds), Time Trial (beat your ghost), Drift Mode (score by sliding), Elimination (last place is out). Local 2-player uses split screen. Remote multiplayer uses the lobby and preset phrases only.
